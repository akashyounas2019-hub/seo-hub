Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cleaning services in Dubai.

Residential cleaning services in Dubai usually include house cleaning, apartment cleaning, villa cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in cleaning, and move-out cleaning. Many companies also offer one-time cleaning, weekly cleaning, monthly cleaning, and recurring cleaning services depending on your needs. Additional services may include sofa cleaning, mattress cleaning, curtain cleaning, and sanitization for homes that need extra care.
Home cleaning services in Dubai focus on routine tasks like dusting, mopping, vacuuming, and surface cleaning in bedrooms, kitchens, and bathrooms. Deep cleaning services go further by targeting hidden dirt, grease, and bacteria in areas like appliance cleaning, hard-to-reach corners, tile and grout cleaning. If your home needs regular upkeep, standard cleaning is ideal. But for heavy dirt buildup or seasonal cleaning, deep cleaning provides a more detailed and long-lasting clean.
A standard deep cleaning package usually covers areas that are not fully cleaned during regular cleaning. It often includes detailed dusting, vacuuming, mopping, bathroom cleaning, kitchen cleaning and sanitization. Deep cleaning may also include tile cleaning, grout cleaning, stain removal, and odor removal. These services are commonly used for apartments, villas, and move-in or move-out situations.
Booking a professional cleaning service in Dubai online is simple and quick. You can book by calling us, sending a WhatsApp message, or filling out our online form. We also offer same-day cleaning for urgent needs, subject to availability. Our support team confirms your booking and schedules a professional cleaner at your preferred time.
Home cleaning rates in Dubai depend on the type of service, property size, and time required. At Safaeewala, rates start from AED 30 per hour for regular house cleaning. Deep cleaning is priced differently as it takes more time and detail. The best way to get an exact rate is to request a quote based on your cleaning needs.
Hiring a maid service in Dubai helps save time and keeps your home clean on a regular schedule. It is a practical choice for busy families, working professionals, and tenants. A maid service can help with dusting, mopping, vacuuming, and general home cleaning. Professional cleaners offer flexible booking, trained staff, and reliable service for apartments, villas, and other residential spaces.
When comparing top cleaning companies in Dubai, look for reviews, trained staff, and clear pricing. A professional cleaning company should offer multiple services, use safe products, and provide clear communication. Experience in residential and commercial cleaning is a key factor in delivering consistent results.
